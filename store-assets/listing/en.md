# AntiMirror — Video Flip

## Summary · 132 characters maximum

Flip one mirrored video horizontally in the current tab—privately, without changing the player controls.

## Detailed description

Fix a mirrored HTML5 video with one deliberate click. AntiMirror flips exactly one main video in
the active tab while the player controls, captions, buttons, and page layout stay in place.

- Manual ON/OFF control for each tab
- Finds videos inside accessible embedded players and web components
- Keeps the selected video pinned until the page or media changes
- Includes a configurable browser shortcut and an EN/RU popup
- Processes everything locally with no analytics, accounts, or external backend

AntiMirror is useful for mirrored recordings, webcam video, streams, classes, demonstrations, and
other HTML5 video that appears horizontally reversed. It does not download, record, or inspect
video frames. Canvas/WebGL players, Picture-in-Picture, protected browser pages, and inaccessible
frames may not be supported.

Open the extension, press **Turn on**, and press it again to restore the original view.
