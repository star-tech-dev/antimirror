AntiMirror privacy notice

Version: 1.0.0 · Effective: September 13, 2026

AntiMirror changes the local display of one video selected by the user. Processing happens inside the browser. The extension has no analytics, advertising SDK, external backend, or account.

Data handled locally

Temporary tab and frame identifiers, random operation tokens, the active phase, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the current top-level URL coordinate the requested action and detect navigation. The original URL is not stored. The selected EN/RU popup language is stored locally as a preference.

Data not collected or transmitted

AntiMirror does not read, record, upload, sell, or transmit video frames, audio, captions, page text, cookies, credentials, browsing history, or media source URLs. It does not send extension data to the developer or third parties.

Site access and control

HTTP/HTTPS access is used to find the selected video in ordinary pages and embedded players. The content agent remains passive until the user presses the popup button or browser shortcut. Turning AntiMirror off removes its effect and active observers. Removing the extension deletes its local language preference.
